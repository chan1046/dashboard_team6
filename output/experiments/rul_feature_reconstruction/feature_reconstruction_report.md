# PHM 2018 FlowCool RUL feature reconstruction (no training)

This report checks the available author manuscript and two PHM Society full papers against the project's source code. “Reproduce” means that the data columns and the published transformation are implementable with Train-only fitting; it does **not** mean that the authors' learned weights, exact scores, or unpublished preprocessing can be recovered. The [paper-by-paper matrix](paper_feature_matrix.csv), [85-feature inventory](existing_85_feature_inventory.csv), [set specification](proposed_feature_sets.csv), and [raw sensor list](raw_sensor_list.csv) are the machine-readable audit.

Primary texts: [Wu et al., *Remaining useful life prediction for ion etching machine cooling system using deep recurrent neural network-based approaches*](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/348862878_Remaining_useful_life_prediction_for_ion_etching_machine_cooling_system_using_deep_recurrent_neural_network-based_approaches), [TV et al., *Recurrent Neural Networks for Online Remaining Useful Life Estimation in Ion Mill Etching System*](https://papers.phmsociety.org/index.php/phmconf/article/download/589/phmc_18_589), [Huang et al., *Remaining Useful Life Estimation for Systems with Abrupt Failures*](https://papers.phmsociety.org/index.php/phmconf/article/download/590/phmc_18_590).

## What the papers actually construct

| Paper | Raw/screening and omitted variables | Additional construction | RUL sequence input and scaling |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wu 2021 | Uses 4-second 20-tool PHM data; its worked RUL example is Tool 01_M02. Keeps FSP=1, removes missing and repeated samples. Removes stage/recipe/recipe-step after mutual-information check; retains FSP among categorical variables. The exact final numeric list and count are **unconfirmed**. | Fits a **normal-data MLP** to estimate pressure from flow: `p_hat=f(q)`, then adds **one** residual `r=p_hat-p`. Published hidden widths 10 and 50, tanh, MSE. The exact normal training partition, initialization, weights, and fitted scaling are **unconfirmed**. | Separate fault-type GRU/GRU-FCs models; lengths F1/F2/F3=45/60/45 observations. A stated preprocessing workflow mentions standardization/normalization but the fitted formula is **unconfirmed**. No explicit manual rolling mean/std/slope/diff input is reported. The RF warning probability is separately smoothed over 150 samples; it is not the MLP residual or a RUL-input rolling statistic. |
| TV et al. 2018 | Keeps FSP=1 and **nine named numeric sensors**, exactly the nine in `raw_sensor_list.csv`; omits the other listed process/sensor variables including stage, Lot, runnum, recipe and timers. | No published added scalar engineered feature. Uses 2,000-observation windows with 500 overlap, downsampling `d1=2`, then time-series-to-vector `d2=10` concatenation. This transforms `(2000,9)` to `(100,90)`; 90 is a reshaped per-step dimension, **not 90 independent original features**. No manual rolling mean/std/slope/diff or MLP feature is reported. | Shared three-output LSTM; paper observes supplied sensors approximately Z-normalized. It clips RUL at `ru=500` in the paper's sample-count target scale and divides by 500. The value must not be copied as 500 seconds/hours. |
| Huang et al. 2018 | Uses sensor measurements, operating-time variables and one-hot FSP for RUL; excludes recipe/recipe-step. Removes missing samples, truncates a run-to-fault sequence at a large gap, then requires ≥3,000 historical observations. The numerical large-gap cutoff and exact included numeric names are **unconfirmed**. | For **early fault detection**, learns normal relationship `q_hat=f(p)` and defines **one** residual `r=q-q_hat`; model family/architecture for `f` is **unconfirmed**. This is the opposite input/output direction and residual sign from Wu. It is **unconfirmed** whether this residual is also an RUL-sequence channel. MLP in this paper is a RUL comparison model, not explicitly the residual generator. No manual rolling mean/std/slope/diff is reported. | Three separate LSTMs; 21 input values per measurement after five-way FSP one-hot. Supplied sensors treated as already normalized. RUL target changes from elapsed time to number of observations remaining; this matters across stops and is not the same as calendar-hour RUL. |

Wu's `p_hat-p` and Huang's `q-q_hat` cannot be exchanged. Both require a Train-only normal reference fit and identical transformation at Validation. The residual *formulas* are reproducible, but their published fitted functions are not. TV's downsample/T2V is a reproducible **sequence transformation**, not a new raw sensor. None of these papers supplies a published “40 EWMA features” or a specified 85-feature construction.

## Current repository comparison

The project Model 1 has **85** input columns listed in `output/baseline_recovery/reproduction_report.json`, constructed in `src/recover_xgb24_baseline.py`. They comprise 9 paper-matching raw numeric sensors; 7 additional current numeric/process values; FSP; 4 categorical/Tool fields; 4 past time/gap fields; 3 process-change flags; 15 within-minute std/min/max values; 40 other historical diff/rate/mean/std/slope/EWMA values; and 2 direct pressure–flow arithmetic values. The detailed row-by-row classification is in `existing_85_feature_inventory.csv`.

**A — directly reusable:** the nine named numeric sensors exist in the raw 4-second parquet and in Model 1. `FIXTURESHUTTERPOSITION` is directly usable for operating-state screening. It becomes constant after FSP=1 filtering, so its usefulness as an RUL model channel must be checked rather than assumed.

**B — new construction:** Wu's single `MLP(flow)-pressure` residual is absent. Huang's reverse `flow-f_hat(pressure)` residual is absent. TV's `d1=2,d2=10` sequence transform is absent from the previous GRU, which sampled every 20th value and therefore discarded the other 19 rather than concatenating 10 of them. These constructions need Train-only fitting and explicit gap policy before experiment; this report creates no feature values or models.

**C — cannot exactly reproduce from publication:** Wu's trained MLP weights, exact normal subset and final numeric channel list; Huang's residual-fitting model, exact sensor list and numeric gap threshold; and detailed paper-specific sequence treatment across machine stops. Mark these **unconfirmed**. A new local implementation can reproduce the published equations and broad selection rules but is not an exact trained-paper replica.

The old 85-feature matrix is a **one-minute** aggregate. Its minute timestamp is the minute's last observation, so its within-minute statistics are causal only when predictions are made at that time. The rolling code groups by a session reset after >120-second minute gap and uses current/past rows. There is no explicit TTF, fault timestamp, next-event time or absolute time among the 85 input names. This source-level check does not establish that every transformed channel is suitable for a **4-second** sequence: 55 rolling/within-minute fields would need to be recomputed on a defined time grid with shutdowns kept distinct. `tool_number`, recipe codes and restart/history fields are causal but can encode Tool/process identity or accumulated operation; they are not automatically comparable across Tools. `pressure_minus_flow` and `pressure_div_flow` are algebraic channels, **not** either learned normal-relation residual.

## Fixed feature sets for the next experiment

| Set | Channels per sequence step | Strength | Constraint |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **A** raw only | **9** | Most faithful to TV's selected numeric inputs and lowest dimensionality. | A sequence still needs valid stop/gap representation; 2,000 operating observations must not silently bridge downtime. |
| **B** raw + Wu-style residual | **10** | Tests one specifically published, physically motivated relation. | Fit MLP only on Train-normal observations, freeze its transform, and report normal-reference definition. Published weights are unavailable. Huang reverse residual can be a separate predeclared 11-channel **detector** ablation, not silently added to RUL B. |
| **C** raw + current Model 1 feature set | **85**, because the nine raw sensors are already within the 85 | Tests existing process and historical signals. | Use the saved 1-minute feature semantics on all three sets, or recompute *all* channels causally at 4-second resolution. Never mix 4-second A/B with 1-minute C while attributing differences only to features. Review Tool identity/categorical encoding separately. |

**Recommended first comparison: A versus B** on the *same timestamp grid, events, gap/stop eligibility and time split*. This isolates one published residual, keeps dimension close to the raw baseline, and avoids the previous experiment's 99.5% cross-stop-window problem. Build valid continuous operating segments or explicit gap-aware windows **before** fitting any RUL model. C is a second, clearly labelled project-engineering comparison; it is not the paper feature set. No model was trained or validation score inspected for this feature choice.
